# MirrorPathAI 🚀

### AI-Powered Identity-Based Alumni Mentorship Platform

> **"Learn from people who were once exactly where you are today."**

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://mirrorpath-ai.vercel.app

---

## Overview

MirrorPathAI helps students discover alumni mentors who align with their aspirations, interests, values, and career goals.

Unlike traditional mentorship platforms that focus on resumes and skills, MirrorPathAI uses AI-powered identity profiling and semantic matching to connect students with alumni whose journeys truly resonate with them.

---

## Problem

Students often struggle with:

- Career uncertainty
- Finding relatable mentors
- Generic career advice
- Limited access to alumni experiences

Meanwhile, valuable alumni knowledge and experiences are often lost after graduation.

---

## Solution

MirrorPathAI transforms alumni experiences into personalized mentorship opportunities through:

- 🧠 AI Identity Profiling
- 🤝 Intelligent Alumni Matching
- 🎯 Personalized Goal Recommendations
- 📈 Goal Tracking & Growth Planning
